Evidence is useful after the original run.

- **Replay** — Reconstruct and compare. Capture, run, minimize, import, export, and compare replay bundles while preserving provenance.
- **Experiments** — Search without losing context. Create, run, cancel, resume, compare, and promote experiments through explicit operation records.
- **Regression** — Make change visible. Plan benchmarks, compare evidence, detect regressions, and promote baselines.
