The site begins where the compiler begins: with one input. From that
input we derive one canonical subject, then follow it through every
observable surface — representation, transformation, execution, and
proof. Each surface is a projection of the same world.

## The proposition

There is exactly one computation in flight on this page. It is the
sample subject. Everything you can see here is a projection of the
canonical state of that subject at the moment the page rendered.

## What you can do

- **Read the chapter sequence** below to follow the computation
  forward. Each chapter is an entity in the world; the order is
  derived from the `order` component.
- **Inspect a claim** in the claims section. Claims are typed
  entities whose `class` and `state` components tell you how much
  weight to give them.
- **Verify a source**. Where a claim points at a source file, the
  link is resolved at build time. A broken link is a build error —
  the architecture is enforced by the compiler, not by reviewer
  discipline.
