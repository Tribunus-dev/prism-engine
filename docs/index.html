03 / WHY PRISM

Deployment policy should be
compiled, not guessed twice.

Conventional runtimes often reconstruct layout, precision, placement, residency, and fallback policy at load or dispatch time. Prism makes those decisions inspectable and seals their contract into a versioned artifact.

TYPICAL RUNTIME

Load weights → derive strategy dynamically → dispatch → expose metrics.

PRISM

Analyze graph → search and admit → seal ComputeImage → transact work → attach evidence.
