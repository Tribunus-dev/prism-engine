06 / Compilation process

Every decision
earns its place.

Prism compiles through ECS-native stages: progressive quantization and ternarization, mixed-precision admission, spatial placement, KV-cache search/compression, and target validation. Candidates are promoted only when the relevant evidence gate passes.

01
INGEST

Understand the source

Weights, tokenizer, graph, and quality contract become an inspectable model identity.

identity→admitted
02
LOWER

Expose the work

Operators become target-neutral execution plans with explicit shapes, dependencies, and boundaries.

graph→plan
03
REPRESENT

Find the safe precision

Each tensor explores progressive quantization, ternarization, mixed precision, and fallback options.

bf16→best fit
04
SEARCH

Measure the frontier

Population candidates compete on quality, latency, memory, residency, and hardware utilization.

24 candidates→frontier
05
REALIZE

Freeze the artifact

Tiles, kernels, DMA, device routes, persistent weights, and KV policy become a versioned CImage.

layout→cimage
06
VERIFY

Prove what will run

Numerical checks, resource legality, receipts, and replay evidence gate publication and execution.

evidence→verified
