# The Physics of Prism

Prism’s interface is a place where computation becomes visible. Its visual behavior follows a small set of laws.

## Light

Light always has a source. A highlight, spectral accent, or caustic must be traceable to an active computation, a selected surface, a chapter transition, or a focused interaction. No ambient rainbow is used as decoration.

## Geometry

Objects keep their silhouette. Depth is expressed through translation, scale, and occlusion; panels do not dissolve into arbitrary glass. A ComputeImage is an object with a spine, layers, identity, and boundary.

## Motion

Motion has inertia. Reveals use one easing family, selection travels through a state change, and information is revealed rather than teleported. Typography moves only when the meaning or active chapter changes.

## Dispersion

Color separates only at a boundary. The default field is nearly monochrome; the chapter accent appears when a surface, route, candidate, provider, or receipt becomes active. Accent colors identify state, not mood.

## Evidence

The interface never turns an illustrative trace into a measured claim. Every object that is illustrative says so, and every evidence surface names its validation boundary.

These laws are the design contract for future Prism frontend work.
