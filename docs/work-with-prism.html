PRISM OBSERVATORY / PARTNERSHIPS

Work with
the computation.

Prism turns hardware reality, deployment intent, and evidence into one inspectable path. Partnerships give that path a real boundary to observe.

Bring a system
worth understanding.

We work with teams that can provide a concrete execution boundary, a meaningful workload, and enough evidence to distinguish what is implemented, measured, and still being explored.

HARDWARE VALIDATION

Make a provider legible.

Prism needs hardware access, runtime documentation, kernels, topology, and reproducible workloads.

Prism delivers
Capability mapping, placement analysis, and a bounded validation report.
Typical duration
2–4 weeks for one provider boundary.
Resulting evidence
Capability surface, legal plan, and explicit evidence boundary.
DATACENTER VALIDATION

Observe the deployment.

Prism needs representative serving requirements, memory and latency constraints, and operational traces.

Prism delivers
An inspectable ComputeImage path, execution receipts, and recovery findings.
Typical duration
4–8 weeks for one serving workload.
Resulting evidence
Route comparison, receipt set, and open findings.
ENGINEERING ENGAGEMENTS

Build the missing system.

RESEARCH COLLABORATIONS

Explore what is not known.

4–12 weeks for a bounded hypothesis.
Resulting evidence

EDGE & ROBOTICS

Carry intent to the edge.

Resulting evidence

Evidence stays honest
at every boundary.
